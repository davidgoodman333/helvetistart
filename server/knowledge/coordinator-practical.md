# Coordinator notes — practical tips for all incoming students (Bern)

Practical guidance from an incoming-exchange coordinator that applies to every
student, whatever their citizenship. These are tips, not rules. Official rules,
deadlines and fees always come from the official sources.

## Getting a Swiss phone number

Get a Swiss phone number early. A local number is useful for registrations,
landlords, the university and banks (many use SMS codes to log in). Prepaid SIM
cards from Swiss mobile providers are the simplest option for an exchange or a
short stay; there is no contract to cancel when you leave.

## Opening a Swiss bank account

A Swiss bank account is mainly needed when you will receive a Swiss salary: an
employer pays wages into a Swiss account. Students who do not work can usually
manage with their account from home. Banks ask who you are and where you live,
so open the account after you have registered with your municipality.
